## What it is
A macOS utility that combined an abbreviation launcher, configurable menus, clipboard history, iTunes control and web search behind hot keys. Freeware turned paid; later retired.

## What was new
User-built "configurations": nested menus of any items, each with its own abbreviation or hot key, so the launcher doubled as a custom menu system.

## What went wrong / limits
- Tried to be everything at once; its configuration window was notoriously dense.
- One-person product that stopped being updated as Quicksilver, then Alfred, took its audience; it was withdrawn rather than modernised.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Avoid feature accretion without a single model; every added source should fit the same row/action shape.

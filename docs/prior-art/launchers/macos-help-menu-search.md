## What it is
Since Mac OS X 10.5 Leopard (2007), the Help menu has a search field (Cmd+Shift+/) that matches every item in the app's menus. Hovering a result opens the real menu and points an animated arrow at the item.

## What was new
- **Search that teaches location**: the result shows where the command lives and its keyboard shortcut, so the next time you do not need search.
- Works in every app for free because menus are system-owned (NSMenu).

## What went wrong / limits
- Nothing notable; limited to menu items, not settings or content.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Take "teach the shortcut": when a launcher row has a keybinding (a panel page, a sway binding from the config), show it on the row.

## What it is
Voice assistant from an acquired SRI spin-off, launched with the iPhone 4S in October 2011 and on the Mac in 2016. Apple announced a context-aware, app-acting Siri with Apple Intelligence in 2024 and in March 2025 said it would take "longer than we thought", delaying it into 2026.

## What was new
Mainstreamed voice assistants years before Alexa and Google Assistant.

## What went wrong / limits
Lost its lead: narrow domains, brittle phrasing, a co-founder saying it was asked to be too many things. The 2024 promises were advertised before they worked, and the retraction damaged trust in the whole Apple Intelligence launch.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Take: do not announce or wire a surface before the thing behind it works; the ROADMAP's research/next split already guards this.

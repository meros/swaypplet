## What it is
A 3D desktop in which icons were physical objects on a tilted desk: toss them, pile them, fan piles out, grow important files. Began as a University of Toronto master's thesis, demoed at TED in 2007, sold for Windows and Mac, discontinued and acquired by Google in 2010.

## What was new
Piles as a casual organisation primitive between "loose" and "folder", with gestures to browse a pile. Physics made the interface feel tactile.

## What went wrong / limits
It organised the desktop icon layer, which most users cover with windows. The novelty of physics wore off; messy desks are messy on screen too. It became a talent acquisition for Android's home screen.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Note: physics feels good in short gestures (a fling that settles), which MOTION.md's springs already cover; it does not make a better organiser.

## What it is
A 2009 video concept: a large multitouch pad below the keyboard (not a touchscreen, to avoid arm fatigue) and Con10uum, a window manager that lays windows out in a single horizontal line, scrolled left and right, with the number of fingers selecting scope (app, window, global). No hardware or software shipped.

## What was new
The linear, scrolling window strip: windows never overlap, new ones append, the viewport pans. Today's scrolling tiling WMs (PaperWM, niri, Hyprland's scrolling layouts) share the idea.

## What went wrong / limits
A concept only; the hardware never existed. Finger-count modes are hard to learn.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Note: the strip idea is alive in niri; for swaypplet, the transferable piece is that a Super+Tab strip of workspaces reads as a continuous row, and the chosen one can grow from its tile (roadmap item 1's tile-to-workspace motion).

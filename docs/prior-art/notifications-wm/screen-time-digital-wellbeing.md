## What it is
Both launched in 2018: usage dashboards, weekly reports, per-app time limits, and bedtime modes. Android's Wind Down turned on DND and switched the screen to grayscale at bedtime.

## What was new
Grayscale as a de-stimulation tool; limits that interrupt rather than just report; bedtime tying DND, grayscale and night light together.

## What went wrong / limits
Limits are one tap from being ignored; reports alone change little. Mostly used for parental control, not self-control.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Avoid: usage tracking dashboards; they add surveillance of the owner for little change in behaviour.
- Adapt: "Wind Down" as a bundle (night light already in process, plus DND) is cheap if quiet modes gain a schedule; not a priority.

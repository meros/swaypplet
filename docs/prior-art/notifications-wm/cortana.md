## What it is
Microsoft's voice assistant, introduced on Windows Phone 8.1 (2014) and put into the Windows 10 taskbar search box (2015). Separated from search in 2019, made an app, and the standalone Windows app ended support in 2023 in favour of Copilot.

## What was new
An assistant integrated with the OS's search box and reminders, with a "notebook" of what it knew about the user that the user could edit.

## What went wrong / limits
Few people talk to a desktop; the taskbar box took space and routed searches to Bing. Windows Phone died, taking the mobile half of the assistant. It was repeatedly repositioned instead of improved.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Avoid: taking permanent bar space for an assistant. Voice on the desktop is a niche.
- Take: the editable "notebook" of what the system knows is the right transparency model for anything that learns.

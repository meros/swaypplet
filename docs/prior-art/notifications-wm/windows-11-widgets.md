## What it is
A flyout from the left of the taskbar with a few widgets (weather, calendar, stocks) above an MSN news feed. The taskbar button shows live weather and, at times, news headlines as a lure. Under the EU Digital Markets Act, EEA users can turn off the MSN feed and links open in the default browser (2024–2025).

## What was new
Little; it is the Live Tiles idea moved to a flyout and monetised through the feed.

## What went wrong / limits
The news carousel visually overshadows the useful widgets; the feed carries ads and clickbait, and the taskbar lure changes at rest to draw attention. The regulated fix (feed off, browser choice) came from law, not design.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Avoid: any content the owner did not ask for in a shell surface; a feed turns a tool into a channel.
- Avoid: a bar item that changes to invite a click (BAR_VISION P1).

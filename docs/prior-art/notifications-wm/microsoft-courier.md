## What it is
A dual-screen booklet tablet with pen and touch, built around a journal: clip content from one page, drop it into a notebook on the other. Leaked by Gizmodo in 2009, cancelled in 2010 because it did not fit Windows strategy. Surface Neo (announced 2019) revisited the form and was also shelved.

## What was new
A "hinge" as a drag target: carry content across by holding it in a shared gutter, a spatial clipboard.

## What went wrong / limits
It ran a custom Windows and did not fit the plan to make Windows the one platform everywhere; it was never a product, so its interaction ideas are untested at scale.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Note: a visible, spatial clipboard (drop zone) is an interesting idea, but swaypplet's clipboard history already serves the need with a keyboard path.

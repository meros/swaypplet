## What it is
A left-edge panel of widgets (weather, calendar, stocks) above an MSN news feed, opened from a taskbar button that also shows live weather. It succeeded Windows 10's "News and Interests" (2021). In 2026 Microsoft began shipping a "quiet by default" version: pinned widgets first, the MSN feed off by default, opening only on click.

## What was new
Little: a glanceable dashboard one click from the taskbar, with third-party widgets from 2023.

## What went wrong / limits
- The feed mixed headlines, sponsored content and trending stories chosen by Microsoft; widely described as noise and as the clearest case of business incentives overriding user intent.
- Opened on hover over the taskbar weather button, so it appeared by accident.
- Taskbar weather and badges put changing content in the bar at rest.
- Microsoft reversed the defaults after five years.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- Avoid: a feed or weather panel. The roadmap's rejection of weather and agenda (BAR_VISION P7) is confirmed by this history.
- Avoid: hover-to-open panels and changing content at rest in the bar.

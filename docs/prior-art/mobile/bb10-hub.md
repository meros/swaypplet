## What it is
BlackBerry 10 (January 2013, BlackBerry Z10) put one unified inbox, the **Hub**, at the far left of its horizontal layout: email, SMS, BBM, social networks, calls and notifications in one list, filterable per account. It was reachable from any app by a gesture (see [bb10-peek](bb10-peek.md)).

## What was new
- **Messages as the centre of the OS**, not a per-app concern: reply from the Hub without opening an app.
- Accounts as filters over one list, not as separate places.
- Always one gesture away, so there was no need to "go to" messages.

## What went wrong / limits
- The platform failed commercially: few apps, a late launch against iOS and Android, and loyal users lost to the keyboard-less Z10. BlackBerry moved to Android phones in 2015 and ended BB10 support.
- Mixing everything into one list makes it long; filtering takes taps.

## Lessons for swaypplet
- **Adapt the Hub as a notification centre that acts**: reply and act inline, grouped by account or task, reached by one key from anywhere.
- The failure was the ecosystem, not the interaction; the idea is still sound for a shell that owns the notification flow.
